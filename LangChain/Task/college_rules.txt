• 1. Mandatory ID Card: It is mandatory for every student to wear their ID card around their neck while on the college campus, in the classroom, and in the library.
• 2. 75% Attendance: As per the university rules , it is mandatory to have at least 75% attendance in both theory and practical classes to appear for the examination .
• 3. Uniform and Dress Code: All students should come to campus in the official uniform prescribed by the college or in decent and formal clothes.
• 4. Punctuality: Students should attend all lectures and practical classes on time. Students arriving late may be denied entry to the class.
• 5. Anti-Ragging: Ragging is strictly prohibited on or off the college campus. If caught doing so, legal action and suspension from the college may be taken.
• 6. Mobile Phone Policy: Mobile phone use is strictly prohibited during classes, practicals and in the library. Phones should be kept silent or switched off during academic hours.
• 7. Behaviour & Discipline: Treat all teachers, non-teaching staff and classmates with respect. Avoid loitering or creating a disturbance in the corridors or academic premises unnecessarily.
• 8. Protection of College Property: Do not damage the college building, desks, computers, library books and other equipment. Writing anything on the walls or desks is prohibited.
• 9. Drug & Alcohol Prohibition: Consumption and possession of tobacco, cigarettes, alcohol or any intoxicants on college premises will be considered a serious offense.
• 10. Cleanliness of the campus: It is everyone's responsibility to keep the college campus clean. Garbage should always be thrown in the dustbin and spitting should not be done anywhere
